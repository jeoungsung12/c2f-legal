1. Purposes of Processing

  • Account identification and save ownership — identifying the account created through Sign in with Apple and linking game progress to it.
  • Cloud save backup and restore — restoring progress after a device change or reinstall.
  • Compatibility and crash fixing — checking device, OS and app version, and analysing crashes.
  • Usage statistics (optional) — only where the user has given optional consent; data is pseudonymised.
  • Delivering tuning values and blocking tampered clients.

The app shows no advertising, performs no tracking, and has no payment features.

How to refuse, and what happens: you may refuse the transfer abroad by declining the required consent on first launch, or — if you already have an account — by deleting your account in the app. If you refuse, cloud save (account features) cannot be provided; you can continue playing with data stored only on your device.

7. Relationship with Apple Inc.

  • Sign in with Apple is an authentication method by which Apple supplies a user identifier to the developer. The developer does not provide any personal data to Apple in this process and does not entrust Apple with processing personal data. Apple's own privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.
  • For beta distribution through TestFlight, tester email addresses and device information are handled by Apple under Apple's own policies. The developer does not separately collect or retain tester email addresses.

8. Destruction Procedure

  • Data is destroyed without delay once the retention period ends or the purpose is achieved.
  • Deleting your account erases the uid, nickname and game save held in the cloud immediately and unlinks Sign in with Apple. Electronic files are deleted irrecoverably.
  • If in-app deletion fails, email the address in section 13 and it will be destroyed manually within 10 days.
  • Analytics and Crashlytics data expire automatically per section 3. Analytics identifiers are reset on account deletion.

9. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal data.

  1. In the app — Settings > Account lets you change your nickname and delete your account directly.
  2. By email — write to onsaemigol@gmail.com; requests are handled and answered within 10 days.

You may act through a legal representative or an authorised agent, who must submit a power of attorney.

10. Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit — all communication uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Access control — database security rules restrict each signed-in user to their own document. Other users' data cannot be accessed.
  • Minimal admin accounts — a single administrative console account, protected by two-factor authentication.
  • Device attestation — Apple App Attest is used to block tampered clients.

12. Children Under 14

The developer does not collect personal data from children under the age of 14. Age is checked on first launch. Where the user is under 14, account features (Sign in with Apple) are not offered, usage statistics and crash reporting are not collected, and tuning values are not fetched. Game data stays on the device and is not sent to the developer.
