Jeong Seong-yun (the "Developer") values users' personal data and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This Policy explains the categories, purposes and retention periods of the personal data processed by Onsaemigol (the "App"), and the rights of users.

1. Purposes of Processing

The Developer processes personal data only for the purposes below and will obtain prior consent if these purposes change.

  • Account identification and save ownership — identifying the account created through Sign in with Apple (iOS) or Sign in with Google (Android) and linking game progress data to that account.
  • Account management — the Google account email address that Firebase Authentication keeps in the account record when you sign in with Google is used only to verify your identity when you send an account deletion or other request by email. The App does not read this value, and it is not used for contact or marketing.
  • Cloud save backup and restore — restoring game progress data after a device change or reinstall.
  • Compatibility checks and bug fixing — checking the device, OS and app version, and analysing the causes of crashes.
  • Usage statistics (optional) — reviewing pseudonymised usage statistics only where the user has given optional consent.
  • Delivering tuning values and blocking abnormal clients — delivering game balance values and blocking access by tampered clients.

The App shows no advertising, performs no user tracking, and has no payment features.

7. Relationship with Apple Inc. and Google LLC

  • Sign in with Apple (iOS) is an authentication method through which Apple provides a user identifier to the Developer. In this process the Developer does not provide users' personal data to Apple and does not entrust the processing of personal data to Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's processing.
  • Sign in with Google (Android) is an authentication method through which Google provides a user identifier to the Developer. In this process the Developer does not provide users' personal data to Google, and receiving the sign-in method itself is not an entrustment of the processing of personal data. Google's privacy policy applies to Google's processing of your Google account. Firebase Authentication keeping the account record after sign-in is the entrustment of processing described in section 5 above.
  • For beta distribution through TestFlight, testers' email addresses and device information are processed by Apple under its own policies. The Developer does not separately collect or retain tester email addresses.
  • For test distribution through Google Play, where testers are specified by an email list, the Developer adds the Google account email addresses of people taking part in the test to the tester list in the Play Console. These email addresses are used only to manage test participation, are kept only in the Play Console tester list, and are removed from the list when the test ends. Testers' installation and device information is processed by Google under its own policies.

8. Destruction Procedure and Method

  • Personal data is destroyed without delay when the retention period ends or the purpose of processing has been achieved.
  • When account deletion is requested, the App immediately requests deletion of the cloud documents containing the uid, nickname and game save, and of the Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google link (the Firebase Authentication account, including the Google account email address, name and profile photo URL). Removal from Google’s live and backup systems follows the procedures and periods in section 3. Electronic files are deleted by a method that makes them unrecoverable. How to remove the App from the connected-apps list on the Apple or Google account side is explained on the account deletion page in section 9 below.
  • If in-app deletion fails, a request sent to the email address in section 13 below will be destroyed manually within 10 days.
  • Analytics and Crashlytics data expire automatically under Google's processing procedures once the periods in section 3 above have passed. The Analytics identifier is also reset upon account deletion.

9. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives, and How to Exercise Them

Users may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Request access to personal data · request correction where there are errors · request deletion · request suspension of processing

There are two ways to exercise these rights:

  1. In the App — under Settings > Account · Data you can change your nickname and delete your account yourself.
  2. By email — send a request to onsaemigol@gmail.com; it will be handled and the result will be sent to you within 10 days.

The account deletion steps, how to request deletion by email without the App, and which data is deleted or kept (and for how long) are set out on the account deletion page.

Users may also exercise their rights through a legal representative or a person to whom they have delegated authority. In that case, a power of attorney must be submitted.

10. Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit — all communications are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Access control — security rules are applied to the cloud database so that a signed-in user can read and write only their own document. Other users' data cannot be accessed.
  • Minimal administrator accounts — there is one administrative console account that handles personal data, and two-factor authentication is applied to it.
  • Device attestation — device attestation based on Apple App Attest (iOS) and Google Play Integrity (Android) is used to block access by tampered clients.

12. Personal Data of Children Under 14

The Developer does not collect personal data of children under the age of 14. The user's age is checked at first launch. If the user is confirmed to be under 14, account (Sign in with Apple / Sign in with Google) features are not provided, usage statistics and crash data are not collected, and tuning values are not fetched. In that case, game data is stored only on the user's device and is not transmitted to the Developer.
